ER-диаграмма базы данных Run Application
==========================================

users
  ├── id (uuid, PK)
  ├── username (text, UNIQUE)
  ├── display_name (text)
  ├── avatar_url (text)
  ├── is_banned (boolean)
  └── created_at, updated_at (timestamptz)

  ├── auth_identities (1:N)
  │     ├── id (uuid, PK)
  │     ├── user_id (FK → users.id)
  │     ├── provider (text: 'email'|'phone')
  │     ├── identifier (text, UNIQUE)
  │     ├── password_hash (text)
  │     └── verified_at (timestamptz)
  │
  ├── refresh_tokens (1:N)
  │     ├── id (uuid, PK)
  │     ├── user_id (FK → users.id)
  │     ├── token_hash (text, UNIQUE)
  │     ├── expires_at (timestamptz)
  │     └── revoked_at (timestamptz)
  │
  ├── runs (1:N)
  │     ├── id (uuid, PK)
  │     ├── user_id (FK → users.id)
  │     ├── status (text: 'draft'|'finished'|'processed'|'failed')
  │     ├── started_at, ended_at (timestamptz)
  │     ├── distance_m (double precision)
  │     ├── elapsed_s, paused_s, moving_s (integer)
  │     ├── points (jsonb) - raw points
  │     ├── track_line (geometry LineString, 4326) - PostGIS
  │     └── processing_error (text)
  │
  │     ├── run_points (1:N)
  │     │     ├── run_id (FK → runs.id, PK part 1)
  │     │     ├── seq (integer, PK part 2)
  │     │     ├── ts (timestamptz)
  │     │     ├── lat, lng (double precision)
  │     │     ├── altitude_m (double precision)
  │     │     ├── accuracy_m (double precision)
  │     │     └── speed_mps (double precision)
  │     │
  │     └── run_pauses (1:N)
  │           ├── id (uuid, PK)
  │           ├── run_id (FK → runs.id)
  │           ├── started_at, ended_at (timestamptz)
  │           └── reason (text: 'manual'|'gps_lost'|'internet_lost')
  │
  ├── territories (1:1)
  │     ├── user_id (FK → users.id, PK)
  │     ├── geom (geometry MultiPolygon, 4326) - PostGIS
  │     └── updated_at (timestamptz)
  │
  ├── user_stats (1:1)
  │     ├── user_id (FK → users.id, PK)
  │     ├── run_count (integer)
  │     ├── total_distance_m (double precision)
  │     ├── total_elapsed_s, total_paused_s, total_moving_s (bigint)
  │     ├── owned_area_m2 (double precision)
  │     └── updated_at (timestamptz)
  │
  └── user_last_notification (1:1)
        ├── user_id (FK → users.id, PK)
        ├── kind (text: 'territory_stolen')
        ├── attacker_user_id (FK → users.id)
        ├── run_id (FK → runs.id)
        ├── stolen_area_m2 (double precision)
        ├── payload (jsonb)
        └── created_at (timestamptz)


Ключевые индексы:
==================
- refresh_tokens: user_id, expires_at, revoked_at
- runs: user_id, status
- run_points: run_id, ts
- run_pauses: run_id, started_at
- territories: geom (GIST индекс для пространственных запросов)


PostGIS функции:
================
1. compute_capture_polygons(track_line, tol_m, min_area_m2)
   → MultiPolygon (захваченные территории)

2. finalize_run_capture(run_id, tol_m, min_area_m2)
   → (capture_area_m2, victims_count)
   - Вычисляет захваченные полигоны
   - Перераспределяет территории (ST_Difference, ST_Union)
   - Обновляет статистику и уведомления
